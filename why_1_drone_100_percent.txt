🔍 WHY YOU SEE "1 DRONE AND 100%" RESULT
============================================

📊 YOUR SETTINGS:
   Grid: 50×50 = 2,500 units
   Drones: 5  
   Radius: 100

📈 THE CALCULATIONS:
   • Coverage per drone: 31,416 units
   • Your 1 drone covers: 12.6× the entire grid!
   • 5 drones would cover: 62.8× the grid!

🤯 WHAT HAPPENS:
   1. System calculates: "1 drone = 1,256% coverage"
   2. System caps it: "1 drone = 100% coverage" 
   3. Algorithm thinks: "Why use 5 drones when 1 gives 100%?"
   4. Result shows: "1 drone, 100% coverage"

💡 THE ALGORITHM LOGIC:
   Since your radius is SO big:
   • 1 drone alone covers the ENTIRE grid 12× over
   • Algorithm optimizes to use minimal drones
   • Why waste 4 extra drones when 1 does the job?

✅ SOLUTION:
   Change radius from 100 → 10
   
   With radius 10:
   • Each drone covers 314 units (reasonable)
   • 5 drones = ~60% coverage (perfect!)
   • Algorithm will use all 5 drones properly

🎯 SUMMARY:
   Your radius is too big → Algorithm uses fewer drones
   Fix radius → Algorithm uses proper number of drones
